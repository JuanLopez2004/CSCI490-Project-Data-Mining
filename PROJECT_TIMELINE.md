**Project:** Heart Failure Prediction  
**Goal:** Beat hospital baseline accuracy of 83.9%  
**Total Duration:** 21 days

**Team:** Cameron + Julian  
**Deliverable:** Clean dataset with insights

- Calculate basic statistics and survival rates
